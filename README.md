## 👋 About me

I'm an **M.Sc. Financial Economics** student at the **Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, Pune**. I use **Python** and **SQL** to work on **forecasting, macro monitoring and equity valuation**, mostly with Indian markets and economic data.

## 🚀 Featured projects

| Project | What I found | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| **📉 Rupee Forecasting Pipeline**<br/>Expanding-window USD/INR backtest | A macro-fundamentals model did **not** beat a random walk (out-of-sample R² about 2% at 1 month and 9% at 3 months); a simple drift model did better (about 15% and 38%) | `Python` `pandas` `statsmodels` |
| **🤖 Time-Series Foundation Models Benchmark**<br/>TimesFM vs ETS, AR, gradient boosting and random walk | TimesFM was most accurate on Nifty 50 volatility (15% lower error, 27% lower quantile loss than a random walk) but the gain was **not statistically significant**; it did not beat drift on USD/INR | `Python` |
| **📊 Macro Risk Monitoring in SQL**<br/>Phillips curve in India, 2019–2024 | **No stable Phillips curve**: slope 0.06 (p = 0.50) over 21 quarters, flipping from −0.83 pre-COVID to +1.85 post-COVID. Flags stagflation, overheating and flat-curve periods | `SQL` |
| **🏭 Godfrey Phillips India: DCF**<br/>Integrated financial model | Base case of ₹1,150–1,540 per share vs a market price near ₹1,930. Terminal value is 75–84% of enterprise value, and method choice matters more than WACC | `Excel` |
| **⚖️ Godfrey Phillips India: Relative valuation**<br/>Comparables and precedent transactions | Market price exceeds every method, but inputs are too thin for a fair value (two Indian peers, a placeholder treasury figure), so it shows where the premium sits, not a price target | `Excel` |
